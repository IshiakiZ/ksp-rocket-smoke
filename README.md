# Rocket Smoke

A rocket's smoke as a true volume: it rolls out of the launch pad's trench at lift-off, climbs after the rocket in a
column, hangs and drifts in the wind, and is lit by the sun and by the flame.

![Smoke coming out of the two ends of the launch pad's trench at lift-off, the column behind a climbing rocket, the trail from afar, and a launch at night](src/RocketSmoke/img/rocket-smoke.jpg)

For Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x. **Needs two other mods**, each a separate download, and does not load without both:
[Keystone](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-keystone) ([on SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone)) and
[Volumetric Explosions](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-volumetric-explosions) 0.5.0 or later
([on SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/4669/Volumetric%20Explosions)), whose smoke this is.

## What it does

* **Smoke that comes out of the flame,** is carried down the jet, spreads, rises and is taken by the wind.
* **Down the trench:** on a launch pad it comes out of the trench's two ends and rolls out over the ground.
* **A trail that stays:** the column stands behind the rocket, a kilometre and more of it, leaning with the wind.
* **What burns shows:** a solid booster's smoke is thick and white, kerosene's thin and grey-brown, and hydrogen
  leaves a little steam.

## Install

Copy the `RocketSmoke` folder from the download into your KSP's `GameData`, beside `Keystone` and
`VolumetricExplosions`.

## Settings

In the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Alt-K; Option-K on a Mac): how much, how long
it hangs, and whether the game's own smoke is hidden.

## Good to know

* Rocket engines only, and only where there is air to hold smoke.
* Made to go with [Engine Flames](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-engine-flames), whose flame lights it.
* It cost next to nothing where it was measured: a seven-engine rocket left the pad at 39 frames a second without it
  and at 38 with it.

## Works on

Windows 11 (Direct3D 11 and OpenGL) and Mac: tried. Linux: never run.
